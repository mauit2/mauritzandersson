Privacy policy

Last updated 9 November 2026

This policy explains what personal data this website processes, why, and what rights you have under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Who is responsible

This is the personal website of Mauritz Andersson, a private individual based in Gothenburg, Sweden. I am the controller for the personal data described here. You can reach me by email at .

Visiting the site: hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, provided by GitHub, Inc. (USA). When you load a page, your browser automatically sends technical data such as your IP address, browser type, the page requested and the time of the request. GitHub processes this data to deliver the site and logs it for security purposes.

Legal basis: my legitimate interest in running a secure, working website (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)). Retention follows GitHub’s privacy statement.

Visiting the site: domain, delivery and security

The domain and traffic run through Cloudflare, Inc. (USA). Cloudflare processes your IP address and request data to deliver pages quickly and to protect the site against bots, scraping and abuse, for example by limiting how many requests a single visitor can make in a short time. Cloudflare may set strictly necessary security cookies, listed in the cookie policy.

Legal basis: legitimate interest in keeping the site secure and available (Article 6(1)(f)). See Cloudflare’s privacy policy.

Statistics: Google Analytics, only with your consent

If you click “Accept” in the cookie banner, the site uses Google Analytics 4, provided by Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland. It helps me understand how many people visit and which pages they read. If you reject or ignore the banner, Google Analytics is never loaded.

With your consent, Google Analytics collects:

  • pages viewed, time on page and how you arrived (for example from LinkedIn or a search engine)
  • how you interact with pages: how far you scroll, which links to other websites you click, and which documents (such as my CV) you download
  • device type, browser, operating system and screen size
  • approximate location (country and city), derived from your IP address. Google Analytics 4 does not log or store IP addresses for visitors in the EU.
  • a random identifier stored in cookies, so repeat visits can be counted

I have switched off Google Signals and advertising features, so the data is not used for ads or to link your activity across devices. Analytics data is kept for 2 months, after which Google deletes it.

Legal basis: your consent (GDPR Article 6(1)(a)), as required for non-essential cookies by the Swedish Electronic Communications Act (lagen (2022:482) om elektronisk kommunikation, chapter 9, section 28). You can withdraw consent at any time via . Withdrawing doesn’t affect processing that happened before. More in Google’s privacy policy.

If you email me

If you contact me by email, I process your email address, your name if you give it, and the content of your message so that I can reply. My email is provided by Google. Legal basis: legitimate interest in answering your message (Article 6(1)(f)). I delete correspondence when it is no longer needed, normally within 12 months of our last exchange, unless we agree otherwise.

People named on this site

This site names people I have worked or studied with, such as co-authors of reports and the authors of my letters of recommendation, together with their roles. They are named with their permission, and their personal contact details have been removed from the published documents. If you are named here and want something changed or removed, email me and I will act on it promptly.

What I don’t do

  • I don’t sell or share your data with anyone for marketing.
  • I don’t run advertising, profiling or automated decision-making.
  • I don’t use social media plugins or embedded content. Links to LinkedIn are plain links, and LinkedIn only receives data if you click through.
  • Fonts are served from this site, not from Google Fonts or another third party.

Transfers outside the EU

GitHub, Cloudflare and Google may process data in the United States. These transfers rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, under which the European Commission considers certified US companies to provide adequate protection, and on the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses where applicable.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • access the personal data I hold about you
  • have inaccurate data corrected
  • have your data erased
  • restrict or object to processing based on legitimate interest
  • receive your data in a portable format
  • withdraw consent at any time

To use any of these rights, email me. I’ll reply within one month. Since Google Analytics data is pseudonymous, I usually can’t link it to you as a person; withdrawing consent and clearing your cookies stops further collection.

You can also complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection, Integritetsskyddsmyndigheten (IMY), or to the supervisory authority where you live.

Changes

If I change how this site handles data, I’ll update this page and the date at the top. If a change affects what you consented to, the cookie banner will ask you again.
