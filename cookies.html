Cookie policy

Last updated 9 November 2026

What cookies are

Cookies are small files a website stores in your browser. Similar technologies, such as your browser’s local storage, work the same way and are covered by this policy too. Under the Swedish Electronic Communications Act, a site may only store non-essential information on your device with your consent. Storage that is strictly necessary to deliver the site, or to remember your choice, doesn’t need consent.

Changing your mind

  • Use , at the bottom of every page, to accept or reject analytics at any time. Rejecting deletes this site’s Google Analytics cookies.
  • You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. If you clear site data, the banner will ask you again.
  • Google offers a browser add-on to opt out of Google Analytics on every site.
